The booth

What the tender says over the set on the night, and nothing more. Ours is a journey; the music does the talking.

“Seven's brought you in from the water. The resident's on the decks: DJ Fab Five Fable, the Snug Harbour. The tide is IN!”

…and one line at the ease, then the awards. Before us, Seven's set asks the floor five things. Answer them dancing:

What have you loved that could not love you back in your language?
What are you willing to be pulled toward tonight?
What trouble taught you its rhythm before you knew its name?
What part of you gets clearer in the dark?
What joy has absolutely no business behaving?
